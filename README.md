## 🧭 Side quests

- **Cold-email a professor** whose paper you read. Ask one specific question. Many research internships start this way.
- **Reproduce a paper** from [Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers) and write up what didn't work.
- **Fix one typo** in the docs of a tool you use. That's your first open-source contribution.
- **Archive something** on the [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) before it disappears.
- **Map your street** on [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/). Real people navigate with it.
- **Teach it back.** Write a blog post on the hardest thing you learned this month.
